FAQ

How long does it take?

About 4 weeks after you approve your mockup.

What photo should I send?

A clear, well-lit photo where the face (or logo) is easy to see. A phone photo is fine.

Can I see it before it's built?

Yes. You'll get up to 5 digital mockups to choose from before I start building.

How does payment work?

You pay a 50% deposit after you pick your mockup, and the rest when your portrait is finished.

Do you ship?

[Shipping details coming soon.] Local delivery in NYC may be possible. [Confirm delivery details.]

What sizes are there?

Small is 300 cubes, Medium is 500 cubes, and Large is 1,000 cubes. Each cube is 3 cm. A 1,000-cube piece is about 120 × 75 cm (47 × 30 in); exact size depends on your image's shape. Custom sizes available, just ask.

Do you make logos for businesses?

Yes. Logo pieces start at $2,500.
